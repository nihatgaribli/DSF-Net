Corpus and generator shift over five seeds and three architectures
==============================================================================
120 evaluations: 3 architectures x 5 seeds x 8 sets

All images are downscaled to 32x32, as CIFAKE itself was built. That removes most of
the high-frequency evidence before any model sees it, and it is the honest comparison
for a 32x32 model. The native-resolution track measures the same shifts separately.

architecture            corpus shift B-A         generator shift C-B
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CIFAKE-CNN   -22.47 pp [-23.67,-21.27]   -19.60 pp [-20.56,-18.65]
  DSF-Net      -23.11 pp [-26.67,-19.55]   -17.11 pp [-19.23,-14.98]
  ResNet-18    -19.70 pp [-20.29,-19.11]   -20.67 pp [-21.41,-19.94]

The corpus term is the larger of the two for 2 of 3 architectures.
A cross-generator number reported without this decomposition therefore attributes to
the generator a loss that is substantially caused by the photographs it is paired
against.
