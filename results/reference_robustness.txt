The decomposition under two choices of reference generator
==========================================================================
  SD 1.5   direct successor of the training generator
  Wukong   same family, deliberately more distant

Terms are percentage points. The difference is |corpus| - |generator|, so a positive
value means the corpus term is the larger of the two. Five seeds throughout.

  detector        corpus  generator  difference  95% half  resolved
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  CIFAKE-CNN      -22.47     -19.60        2.87      2.07  yes
  DSF-Net         -23.11     -17.11        6.00      5.46  yes
  ResNet-18       -19.70     -20.67       -0.97      0.82  yes
  CLIP probe      -28.71      -8.61       20.10      0.95  yes

Wukong
  detector        corpus  generator  difference  95% half  resolved
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  CIFAKE-CNN      -22.01     -20.14        1.87      2.37  no
  DSF-Net         -22.57     -17.74        4.83      7.45  no
  ResNet-18       -19.22     -21.23       -2.01      2.50  no
  CLIP probe      -33.65      -2.85       30.80      0.49  yes

What survives the substitution
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  direction  : 4 of 4 detectors keep the sign of the
               difference, so which term dominates is a property of the detector and
               not of the reference. The sign change across detectors, which is the
               central claim, is present under both references.
  resolution : 1 of 4 stay individually resolved at five seeds (CLIP probe).
               3 lose it: CIFAKE-CNN, DSF-Net, ResNet-18.

Why the numbers move, exactly
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Write a for the in-distribution accuracy, r for the reference set and m for the mean
of the remaining generators. The corpus term is r - a and the generator term is
m - r. Both are negative here and r lies between a and m, so

    |corpus| - |generator|  =  (a - r) - (r - m)  =  a + m - 2r

Checked against every detector, seed and reference above: largest discrepancy 1.4e-14
percentage points, which is floating point and not approximation.

The reference sits on the boundary between the two terms, so it enters the reported
difference twice and with a coefficient of exactly -2. A reference one point easier
for a detector moves that detector's reported difference two points toward its
generator term. Nothing migrates; the boundary moves.

That is the whole of the table above. Per detector, the reference accuracies are:

  detector            A   SD 1.5   Wukong   shift   difference
  CIFAKE-CNN      94.65    72.18    72.64    0.46        -1.00
  DSF-Net         95.41    72.30    72.84    0.54        -1.17
  ResNet-18       97.12    77.42    77.90    0.48        -1.04
  CLIP probe      95.11    66.40    61.46   -4.94        10.70

Wukong is a little easier than SD 1.5 for the three convolutional detectors and
considerably harder for the probe, and every movement in the difference follows from
that at roughly twice the size and the opposite sign, as the identity requires.

What this means for anyone using the decomposition
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The reference is not a free parameter. It must be chosen by proximity to the training
generator, which is why this study uses SD 1.5, and it must be reported, because a
reader cannot reconstruct the split without knowing where the boundary was put. The
sensitivity is not a defect of the method so much as a property of any decomposition
that splits a total at an intermediate point: the point is load bearing.

It also sets a practical floor. With a five-seed interval of two to seven points on
the convolutional detectors, a reference chosen half a point away from the ideal one
costs a point of the difference, which is inside the noise. A reference chosen five
points away, as Wukong is for the probe, is not.

Reported because we ran it. A robustness check that weakens a result belongs in the
record exactly as much as one that confirms it.
