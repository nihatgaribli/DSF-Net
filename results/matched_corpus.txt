The corpus term before and after matching on twelve summary statistics
==========================================================================
One-to-one nearest-neighbour matching on the propensity score fitted from the twelve
statistics, caliper 0.2 standard deviations of its logit. Unmatched images are
dropped rather than stretched.

set   pair                          n before  n after  screen before  screen after
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A     A_real vs A_fake                  1000      634         0.6730        0.4392
B     imagenet_real vs gen_SD15          500      437         0.6180        0.4920

Neither matched set screens above 0.4920, so a model reading only these
twelve statistics can no longer tell the classes apart in either of them, and
what remains of the corpus term is not something they can explain. One of the
two screens below 0.5, which is a weak model generalising worse than guessing
once the signal it had is gone, not a signal in the other direction.
