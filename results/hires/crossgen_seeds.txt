The decomposition at 256x256, resolution against generator
==========================================================================
A 256px real vs seen generators. B the same images cropped to 128px. C 128px real
vs BigGAN, never seen in training. B - A is the resolution term, C - B the generator
term. The real photographs are identical across all three sets.

detector      seeds   resolution   95% half   generator   95% half  |res|-|gen|
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
dsfnet            5        -9.59       1.07      -18.01       8.37        -8.42
resnet18          1       -14.90        nan      -21.55        nan        -6.65

For DSF-Net over 5 seeds the generator term is the larger, by 8.42 points
with a 95 per cent half-interval of 8.50, so the ordering is not resolved.

What is resolved here is the asymmetry between the two terms rather than their
ordering. The resolution term is small and tight, and the generator term is twice
as large and five times as variable across seeds, which is the same instability
the 32x32 track reports for the magnitude of its split.

The point estimate does reverse. At 32x32 DSF-Net's corpus term is the larger of
its two by 6.00 points; here the generator term is the larger by 8.42. If that
reversal held at five seeds it would say the decomposition depends not only on the
detector but on which nuisance the intermediate set holds fixed. It does not hold
at five seeds, so it is reported as an estimate and not as a finding. Resolving it
would need more seeds at 256px, which is the one thing this track is expensive in.

What the track does establish is that the operation is not specific to CIFAKE
against ImageNet at 32x32. The same three-set construction separates two terms at
eight times the resolution, on a different corpus, against a different held-out
generator, and returns a resolution term of -9.59 with an interval of 1.07.

ResNet-18 has one high-resolution checkpoint, so its row is a single run with no
interval and is not compared against the seeded row.
