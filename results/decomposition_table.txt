The difference between the two terms spans 21.1 points across four detectors,
from -0.97 on ResNet-18 to +20.10 on CLIP probe, every one of them resolved.
It changes sign. A cross-generator number is therefore not decomposable in general;
it is decomposable only for a named detector, and reporting one without saying which
detector produced it leaves the reader unable to attribute the loss at all.

The ordering also tracks what each detector reads. The probe on frozen CLIP features
is hurt overwhelmingly by new photographs and barely by a new generator, which is
what a semantic representation should do. The fine-tuned ResNet-18 is the only one
where the generator term is the larger of the two.
